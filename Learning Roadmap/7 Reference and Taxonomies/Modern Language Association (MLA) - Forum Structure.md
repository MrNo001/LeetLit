# Modern Language Association (MLA): Forum Structure

> Literature's closest equivalent to the PhilPapers taxonomy. The MLA organizes the profession into **forums**, which replaced its older divisions and discussion groups (a change announced in 2014); each forum gets guaranteed sessions at the annual convention. The structure shows that literary specialization is a **matrix**: a language or region crossed with a period, overlaid with genre, theory and method, and interdisciplinary lenses.

## The 9 categories
| Category | Forums | What it slices by | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LLC: Languages, Literatures, and Cultures** | 78 | Language/region × period | see the English and American breakdown below; also French (7), German (4), Spanish and Iberian (5), Latin American (6), Chinese (3), Japanese (2), Arabic, Korean, Hebrew, Yiddish… |
| **CLCS: Comparative Literary and Cultural Studies** | 20 | Cross-language periods and regions | Medieval · Renaissance and Early Modern · 18th-Century · Romantic and 19th-Century · 20th- and 21st-Century · Global Anglophone · Global South · Caribbean · Gothic Studies · Arthurian · Classical and Modern |
| **GS: Genre Studies** | 9 | Genre | Poetry and Poetics · Prose Fiction · Drama and Performance · Life Writing · Children's and Young Adult · Comics and Graphic Narratives · Speculative Fiction · Folklore, Myth, and Fairy Tale · Travel Writing |
| **TM: Theory and Method** | 7 | Method | Literary and Cultural Theory · Literary Criticism · Bibliography and Scholarly Editing · Book History, Print Cultures, Lexicography · Language Theory · Libraries and Research · The Teaching of Literature |
| **TC: Transdisciplinary Connections** | 21 | Interdisciplinary lens | Philosophy and Literature · Psychology, Psychoanalysis, and Literature · Marxism, Literature, and Society · Postcolonial Studies · Women's and Gender Studies · Sexuality Studies · Race and Ethnicity Studies · Ecocriticism and Environmental Humanities · Religion and Literature · Science and Literature · Translation Studies · Adaptation Studies · Digital Humanities · Disability Studies · Memory Studies · Medical Humanities · Law and the Humanities · Cognitive and Affect Studies · History and Literature · Anthropology and Literature · Popular Culture |
| **MS: Media Studies** | 4 | Medium | Screen Arts and Culture · Visual Culture · Sound · Opera and Musical Performance |
| **LSL: Language Studies and Linguistics** | 8 | Linguistics | Applied Linguistics · Language Change · Language and Society · Linguistics and Literature · Second-Language Teaching… |
| **RCWS: Rhetoric, Composition, and Writing Studies** | 5 | Writing | Creative Writing · History and Theory of Rhetoric · History and Theory of Composition · Literacy Studies · Writing Pedagogies |
| **HEP: Higher Education and the Profession** | 3 | Profession | Community Colleges · Part-Time and Contingent Faculty · Teaching as a Profession |

Counts are from the 2022 program index; forums are added and reorganized over time.

## The period spine (LLC English and American)
| English (11 forums) | American (13 forums) |
|---|---|
| Old English → Middle English → **Chaucer** → 16th-Century → **Shakespeare** → 17th-Century → Restoration and Early-18th-Century → Late-18th-Century → English Romantic → Victorian and Early-20th-Century → 20th- and 21st-Century English and Anglophone | Early American → 19th-Century → Late-19th- and Early-20th-Century → 20th- and 21st-Century; plus African American · Asian American · Chicana and Chicano · Latina and Latino · Indigenous Literatures of the US and Canada · Jewish American · Italian American · Southern US · US literatures in languages other than English |

Only two single authors have their own forum: **Chaucer** and **Shakespeare**. The English period bands closely match the Norton Anthology's six volumes and Oxford's period papers.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **Literature needs multi-axis tags, not one tree.** Every text node can carry *language/region*, *period*, *genre* and optional *lens* tags (TC/TM). Specialization paths are then slices of the matrix: "Victorian novel," "postcolonial Anglophone," "ecocriticism across periods."
- **Period bands are consistent across sources** (Norton, Oxford, MLA), so they make a stable backbone for a literature roadmap.
- **The TC lenses match the theory schools** in Eagleton and Paul Fry's ENGL 300 (Marxism, psychoanalysis, gender, race, postcolonial). The theory track and the specialization taxonomy can share one vocabulary.
- **Breadth is enormous:** 78 language and period forums. A Western-canon-first MVP should still reserve the language/region axis so non-English literatures can be added without restructuring.
