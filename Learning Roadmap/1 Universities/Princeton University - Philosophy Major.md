# Princeton University: Philosophy Major

> Princeton combines a light distribution rule with heavy **independent writing**: a junior paper and a year-long senior thesis.

## Requirements
| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Courses | At least **8** philosophy courses |
| Distribution | 6 of the 8 must cover **two courses in three of the four areas**: (1) metaphysics, (2) ethics and philosophy of value, (3) logic and philosophy of science, (4) history of philosophy. The other 2 are unrestricted |
| Junior year | Junior independent-work group, then a **spring junior paper** (an older departmental guide says normally at least ~5,000 words) |
| Senior year | **Senior thesis:** a year-long research project under a faculty adviser. Third-party sources report 10,000–15,000 words and an oral exam (unverified) |

## Pedagogical note
Princeton lectures are paired with **precepts** (small discussion sections led by faculty or graduate students). The two-tier pattern of lecture for content and precept for argument practice is standard across US programs.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **"Two in three of four areas"** is a flexible completion rule that balances breadth and choice. It suits "certificates" or "path completion" badges.
- **Research capstone:** the junior paper and senior thesis show where structured paths hand off to self-directed inquiry. LeetLit's advanced tier could end in a capstone essay rather than more items.
