# Philosophy Bites (Warburton and Edmonds)

> Since 2007: **411 episodes and 34M+ downloads** of 15–20-minute interviews in which a leading philosopher (Peter Singer, Martha Nussbaum, David Chalmers…) explains *one* topic. It is "bite-sized" philosophy from the people who work on it.

## The path
- **No path.** Each episode stands alone. The archive works like an encyclopedia of expert micro-lectures across ethics, mind, political philosophy, aesthetics, history of philosophy and applied topics.
- **Compiled into books** (OUP): *Philosophy Bites* (2010), *Philosophy Bites Back* (2012, on historical figures), *Philosophy Bites Again* (2014).
- The same team has made themed spin-offs (for example *Ethics Bites* with the Open University). Warburton also wrote *A Little History of Philosophy* (see `6 Books/`).

## Episode anatomy
1. The topic is named ("Today's topic is…") along with the guest's credentials.
2. A definitional opening question.
3. The core position or argument is laid out.
4. Objections are put by the interviewer, followed by replies.
5. Done in ~15 minutes. There is no padding.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **The 15-minute expert explainer** is the right size for a LeetLit "concept video": definition → argument → objection → reply.
- **The interview format guarantees an objection step.** LeetLit explainers could build in a mandatory "strongest objection" section.
