# History of Philosophy Without Any Gaps (Peter Adamson)

> The most complete free history of philosophy in any medium: **~770 episodes across 10 series**. It is weekly, ~20 minutes each, and it moves slowly enough to cover figures the canon skips.

Companion books (OUP) turn each series into a volume, for example *Classical Philosophy*, *Philosophy in the Hellenistic and Roman Worlds*, *Philosophy in the Islamic World*, *Medieval Philosophy*, *Byzantine and Renaissance Philosophy*, *Classical Indian Philosophy*, and *Philosophy in the Reformation*.

## Episode anatomy
- **Solo episodes (~20 min):** a tightly scripted narrative with one idea per episode, wry humor, and a recap and preview at the ends. Adamson reconstructs the argument and then situates it.
- **Interview episodes (every few episodes):** a conversation with a specialist on the same figure. This is a "second opinion" layer.
- Feeds and downloadable zips for each series. Episode pages list further reading (primary and secondary).

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **"Without any gaps" is the opposite of a Blind-75 list.** It is the complete graph. LeetLit could use HoPWaG's sequence as the full chronological backbone and then *highlight* a curated core path through it.
- **Non-Western traditions as parallel series** (Islamic, Indian, Africana, Chinese) show how to structure a global philosophy roadmap: separate timelines with crosslinks, not a single merged line.
- **20 minutes per episode** confirms the popular "one concept per short session" granularity.
