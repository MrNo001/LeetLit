# The Lonely Palette (Tamar Avishai)

> "Returns art history to the masses, one object at a time." Since 2016 there have been 71 episodes plus bonuses, each a slow look at **one painting or object**.

## Episode anatomy
1. **Vox pops:** unsuspecting museum visitors are recorded reacting to the artwork in front of them, with their honest, unschooled first impressions.
2. **Deep dive:** the host walks through the object (what you see), the artist, the movement, and the social context.
3. **Return to looking:** the listener now sees what the visitors couldn't.
- 30–40 minutes, a single object, no visuals (the listener is invited to pull up the image).

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **Start from naive reactions, then add expertise.** This is the art version of Sandel's "intuition first." A LeetLit art item could ask learners for their first impression before showing any context, then show a community distribution of reactions.
- **One object, deep, and audio-only** proves that art learning can work without visual-heavy UI, provided the learner has the image open alongside.
