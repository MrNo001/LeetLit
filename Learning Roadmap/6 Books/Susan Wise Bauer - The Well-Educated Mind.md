# Susan Wise Bauer: *The Well-Educated Mind*

> A self-study program for adults who missed a classical education. Each genre has its own **annotated chronological list**, read with a **three-stage method** (grammar → logic → rhetoric) and a reading journal.

## The method (trivium)
| Stage | Classical name | What the reader does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Grammar** (first reading) | Understand: note the title and author, summarize each chapter in a few sentences, list characters or key terms, give the book a provisional title |
| 2 | **Logic** (second reading) | Evaluate: analyze structure and technique, the author's argument or the novel's devices, and what the author is trying to do and whether they succeed |
| 3 | **Rhetoric** (discussion) | Express: form your own opinion and argue it in writing or with a reading partner |

The **reading journal** is mandatory: summaries, quotations, questions and evaluations.

## The paths (genre lists, each read chronologically)
| Genre | Range of the list (from publisher descriptions) |
|---|---|
| **Fiction** (novel) | Cervantes, *Don Quixote* → … → Cormac McCarthy |
| **Autobiography** | from Augustine's *Confessions* onward |
| **History** | Herodotus → … → Laurel Thatcher Ulrich |
| **Drama** | from the Greek tragedians onward |
| **Poetry** | from the ancient epics onward |
| **Science** (added in the 2015 expanded edition) | Hippocrates → … → the asteroid that ended the dinosaurs |

Each genre chapter teaches **genre-specific questions** before its list (for example, for fiction: who is the central character, what do they want, what stands in the way?). The original 2003 edition covered five genres, and the 2015 edition added science and 21st-century titles.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **Three passes per work is a natural three-tier exercise set:** Grammar = comprehension questions (Easy), Logic = analysis questions (Medium), Rhetoric = argued response (Hard).
- **Genre-specific question banks** ("questions for reading a novel," "for reading history") are reusable templates for every text in a genre.
- **Parallel genre tracks, each chronological,** are another way to slice the literature roadmap besides period bands (Oxford) and skills spirals (AP).
