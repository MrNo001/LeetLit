# Terry Eagleton: *Literary Theory: An Introduction*

> A standard first book on theory, and openly partisan. It tours the major schools, then argues that literary theory has always been political and should say so.

## The path (chapters)
| # | Chapter | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | **What Is Literature?** | Russian Formalism and "defamiliarization." Eagleton argues "literature" is a historically variable value judgment, not a fixed category |
| 1 | **The Rise of English** | How English became a university subject: Arnold, F. R. Leavis and *Scrutiny*, I. A. Richards, Empson, American New Criticism, Northrop Frye |
| 2 | **Phenomenology, Hermeneutics, Reception Theory** | Husserl, Heidegger, Gadamer; reader-response (Iser, Jauss, Fish) |
| 3 | **Structuralism and Semiotics** | Saussure, Lévi-Strauss, Propp, Jakobson, Genette's narratology, Barthes |
| 4 | **Post-Structuralism** | Derrida and deconstruction, late Barthes, Foucault, the Yale critics (de Man) |
| 5 | **Psychoanalysis** | Freud, Lacan, Kristeva |
| Concl. | **Political Criticism** | All criticism is political; proposes studying "discourse" or rhetoric instead of a privileged "literature" |
| Afterword | (2nd ed., 1996) | Developments since 1983, including feminist and cultural theory |

## Editions
- **1983:** first edition.
- **1996:** second edition, with a new preface and an afterword covering later developments in feminist and cultural theory.
- **2008:** 25th-anniversary edition with a new preface by Eagleton (some listings call it a foreword).

## How a chapter works
- Historical situation of the school → its key thinkers and concepts → worked readings of literary examples → Eagleton's critique of the school's blind spots, usually its politics.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **Theory "lenses" are a second axis.** The same text can be read through formalist, reader-response, structuralist, deconstructive, psychoanalytic and political lenses. A lens track can be paired with any literature node (compare Paul Fry's ENGL 300).
- **A survey with a thesis:** Eagleton shows that the curator's argument is part of the content. LeetLit could present the debate *about* each lens, not only its definition.
- **Theory curricula date fast.** The book needed an afterword for feminist and cultural theory and later editions for newer movements, so LeetLit content needs versioning.
