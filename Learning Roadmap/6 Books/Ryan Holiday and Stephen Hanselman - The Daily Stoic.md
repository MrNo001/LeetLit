# Ryan Holiday and Stephen Hanselman: *The Daily Stoic*

> Popular philosophy as a **daily habit**: one Stoic quotation and a page of commentary for every day of the year, organized into three disciplines and twelve monthly themes. It turned one ancient school into a mass-market practice.

## How a day works
- **Title:** a one-line principle.
- **Quotation:** a short passage, mostly from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca or Epictetus, with some earlier Stoics, in translations by Hanselman.
- **Commentary:** about a page by Holiday applying it to modern life.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **A calendar can carry real philosophy to a mass audience.** Themed months plus one item a day is the same habit loop as the Harvard Classics' 1916 daily reading guide.
- **Practice, not just survey:** each entry ends by applying the idea to the reader's life. Ethics paths could pair "understand the argument" items with "apply it" reflections.
- **Depth trade-off:** one school, quotation-sized fragments and heavy commentary. It works as an on-ramp, not a curriculum leading to primary texts.
