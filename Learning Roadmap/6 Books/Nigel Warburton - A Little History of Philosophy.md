# Nigel Warburton: *A Little History of Philosophy*

> Forty bite-size chapters, **each a thinker plus a hook question,** taking a beginner "on a chronological tour of the major ideas in the history of philosophy" from Socrates to Peter Singer. Warburton also co-hosts *Philosophy Bites*.

## Chapter anatomy
- Hook question or scene → life sketch → one central idea → one objection → a **handoff sentence** that sets up the next chapter's thinker. Each chapter takes about 10 minutes to read.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **The "one thinker, one question, one objection, ten minutes" unit** is the ideal *Easy* node in a history-of-philosophy path.
- **The 20th-century tail** (Foot and Thomson, Rawls, Turing and Searle, Singer) connects history to the problems-first curriculum. It works as a bridge between LeetLit's history path and its problems path.
