# Coursera (UPenn): ModPo *Modern & Contemporary American Poetry*

> A cult-classic MOOC (**~104,000 learners, 4.8★**) whose signature is the **collaborative close reading**: Al Filreis and a table of TAs (often with the poet) spend 10–20 minutes on *one short poem*, line by line, thinking out loud.

## The path (10 weeks)
| Week | Module | Poets (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whitman and Dickinson, two proto-modernists | Dickinson, "I dwell in Possibility," "Tell all the truth but tell it slant"; Whitman, "Song of Myself" |
| 2 | Whitmanians and Dickinsonians | W. C. Williams, Ginsberg, Niedecker, Armantrout (**Essay 1**) |
| 3 | Rise of modernism: imagism; Williams | H.D., Pound ("In a Station of the Metro"), Stevens ("Thirteen Ways…"); Williams ("The Red Wheelbarrow," "This Is Just to Say") (**peer review 1**) |
| 4 | Gertrude Stein; modernist edges | Stein's *Tender Buttons*; Baroness Elsa; Tzara, "To Make a Dadaist Poem" (**Essay 2**) |
| 5 | 1930s poets; Harlem Renaissance; Frost; 1950s formalism | Cullen, McKay, Hughes, Brooks; Frost, "Mending Wall"; Wilbur |
| 6 | The Beats | Ginsberg, "Howl"; Kerouac; Creeley; Baraka |
| 7 | The New York School | O'Hara, Koch, Ashbery, Guest, Schuyler, Mayer, Myles (**Essay 3**) |
| 8 | Language poetry | Hejinian, *My Life*; Perelman; Bernstein; Howe; Mullen (**peer review 3**) |
| 9 | Chance poetry | Cage, Mac Low, Retallack (**Essay 4**) |
| 10 | Conceptualism and unoriginality | Bök, *Eunoia*; Bergvall; Jordan Abel (**peer review 4**) |

## Session anatomy
- **Video close reading (core):** a poem on screen, a group around a table, then line-by-line discussion, with disagreements left visible. Many videos include the poet.
- **Readings:** the poems themselves plus audio of poets reading (some with text–audio alignment).
- **Live webcast (~90 min, weekly):** learners call in and post questions in real time.
- **PoemTalk** (a ~30-min podcast with the Poetry Foundation) for extra close readings.
- **Community Collaborative Close Readings:** learners worldwide film their own group readings.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **"Show the reading, not the answer."** ModPo models the *process* of interpretation (tentative, revisable, social). LeetLit's "solution videos" for poems should look like this rather than lectures.
- **Short texts make high-volume practice possible:** a poem is an ideal "Easy/Medium problem" unit.
- **Peer review of essays** scales feedback, and ModPo's certificate required 16 peer reviews.
