# The Great Courses: *Great Minds of the Western Intellectual Tradition*

> The flagship **adult continuing-education** philosophy course: an 84-lecture chronological march from the Presocratics to late-20th-century thought, team-taught by 12 professors. This is "the lecture" in its purest commercial form.

## The path
- **Scale:** 84 lectures × ~30 min (≈43 hours). First released 2000, now in its 3rd edition.
- **Arc:** Presocratics → Socrates, Plato, Aristotle → Hellenistic and Roman → Biblical and early Christian thought → medieval synthesis (Augustine, Aquinas) → Renaissance and Reformation → Scientific Revolution → Enlightenment (Descartes → Kant) → 19th century (Hegel, Marx, Mill, Darwin, Nietzsche) → existentialism → 20th-century thought (to Derrida and beyond).
- **Packaging:** sold in parts of ~12 lectures, each with a guidebook section (lecture summaries, glossary, biographical notes, questions). The full guidebook runs ~421 pages.
- **Faculty:** Alan Charles Kors, Darren Staloff, Dennis Dalton, Douglas Kellner, Jeremy Adams, Jeremy Shearmur, Kathleen M. Higgins, Louis Markos, Mark Risjord, Phillip Cary, Robert C. Solomon, Robert H. Kane.

## Lecture anatomy
- **Monologue lecture** with an outline: biography and context → core ideas → influence → assessment. Typically one thinker per lecture.
- The guidebook mirrors each lecture as an outline plus "questions to consider."

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **One-thinker-per-30-min** is the default granularity of popular philosophy instruction (as in HoPWaG, Philosophize This!, and Durant). It is a natural size for a LeetLit "concept video."
- **Lecture plus outline plus questions** is a minimal, proven study packet.
- **Team-taught by specialists** means more authority but uneven style. LeetLit should standardize a template across contributors.
