# Smarthistory and Khan Academy: Art History

> The default free art-history textbook of the internet: **880+ videos and 2,000+ essays by 200+ contributors**, covering the Paleolithic to the present. It is the official art-history content of Khan Academy and the main free resource for AP Art History's 250 works.

## The path
- **Organized like a survey textbook:** by era and region (prehistory → ancient Mediterranean → medieval Europe and Byzantium → Renaissance → Baroque → 18th–19th c. → modernism → contemporary), plus Asia, Africa, the Islamic world, the Americas and the Pacific as their own sections.
- **AP Art History guide:** every one of the College Board's 250 required works has an essay and/or video, grouped by AP content area. This makes Smarthistory the de facto companion to the AP list.
- **Foundations section:** how to look, art-historical vocabulary, materials and techniques, before the chronology.

## Session anatomy (the Smarthistory video)
- **Two art historians in conversation, standing in front of the real object** (often Beth Harris and Steven Zucker). It is unscripted and conversational, with the camera moving to details as they talk.
- Typical flow: what do we see? → what is it made of, and how? → who made it, for whom, and why? → what changed because of it?
- Length is usually 4–15 minutes per work. Essays (peer-reviewed) give the citable version.
- History: started in **2005** as audio guides for the Met and MoMA. It won a Webby (Education) in 2009 and is funded partly by the NEH.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **"Two experts in front of the work"** is the art equivalent of ModPo's table discussion. It models looking as a dialogue, not a lecture.
- **A per-work content unit already exists for all 250 AP works.** LeetLit could link to or license this rather than re-create it, and build exercises (attribution, comparison) on top.
- **Foundations first** (how to look, vocabulary, techniques) then chronology mirrors the "skills paper first" pattern in Oxford English and AP Lit.
