# Crash Course Literature

> John Green's literature series (45 episodes) teaches **how to read** through the books students are actually assigned in school. It is effectively a companion to the US high-school and AP Lit canon.

## Episode anatomy
- **Part 1 of a book:** context (author, era) → plot setup → how the language works (e.g. "Of Pentameter & Bear Baiting" on *Romeo and Juliet*'s verse).
- **Part 2:** themes and interpretive debates (e.g. "Was Gatsby Great?", "Race, Class, and Gender in *To Kill a Mockingbird*").
- **Thought Bubble** animates a key scene or a historical context.
- Season 1 opens with **"How and Why We Read,"** a meta-episode on reading as a skill before any book.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **The two-part split (understand the text → interpret it)** matches Bauer's "grammar → logic" stages and the summary/analysis split in *Literature and History*.
- **A school-canon entry point:** many users arrive having been assigned *Gatsby* or *Macbeth*. A "Books you read in school, revisited" path is a strong onboarding hook.
- **A meta-episode first** ("How and Why We Read") again confirms a reading-skills node at the root of the literature tree.
