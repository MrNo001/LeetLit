# Great Art Explained (James Payne)

> Breaks down **one famous artwork per ~15-minute video** "in easily-understandable, non-art speak." It covers the work itself, the artist's life, and the work's place in the artist's career and in art history.

## Episode anatomy
1. **The hook:** the work's fame or a mystery about it.
2. **Biography:** the artist's life up to the moment of making.
3. **Close looking:** zoomed details of composition, technique, symbolism and hidden elements.
4. **Context:** patrons, politics, the movement.
5. **Legacy:** reception, influence, the work's afterlife.

The book version, *Great Art Explained: The Stories Behind the World's Greatest Masterpieces*, covers 30 works, from Botticelli and Hilma af Klint's altarpieces to Monet's *Water Lilies* and Georgia O'Keeffe.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **The single-work deep dive** is the dominant popular format for art, as in *The Lonely Palette* and Smarthistory. That supports a "work card" as LeetLit's atomic art unit.
- **A five-beat template** (hook → biography → looking → context → legacy) can standardize LeetLit's art explainers.
