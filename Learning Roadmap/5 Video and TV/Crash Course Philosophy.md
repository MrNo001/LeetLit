# Crash Course Philosophy

> **46 episodes × ~10 minutes.** It is the most-watched intro philosophy series on YouTube, built as a **problems-first syllabus**: reasoning tools first, then knowledge, God, mind, language, art and ethics.

## Episode anatomy (~10 min)
1. **Cold open:** a hook from pop culture (Batman, *The Matrix*, *Inception*, Indiana Jones).
2. **Concept:** the argument stated in standard form, with key terms on screen.
3. **Thought Bubble / Flash Philosophy:** an animated thought experiment such as the trolley problem, the Ship of Theseus, or brain in a vat.
4. **Objections and replies,** quickly.
5. **Recap** of what we learned and a preview of the next episode.

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **Reasoning tools come first** (episodes 1–3, deduction, induction, abduction), exactly as AQA's cross-cutting skills and Cambridge's Formal Methods do. LeetLit's philosophy roadmap should start with an argument-skills node.
- **Pop-culture hooks** lower the barrier, and each episode is a single portable concept: a good "Easy" tier.
- **Animated thought experiments** could become interactive items (vary the case and see whether your intuition flips).
