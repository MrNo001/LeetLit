# Crash Course Art History

> 22 episodes launched April 2024. Unlike survey courses it is **organized by questions, not periods** ("What makes an artist 'great'?", "Should we separate art from the artist?"), using works from across eras and cultures as evidence.

## The path (22 episodes)
1 Why We Study Art · 2 How to Look at Art · 3 The History of Museums · 4 What Makes an Artist "Great"? · 5 Should We Separate Art from the Artist? · 6 What Is Good Art? · 7 How Does Art Tell Stories? · 8 How Do Religions Use Art? · 9 The Hidden Meanings in Nature Art · 10 How Communities Make Art · 11 Bodies in Art · 12 Origins of Color (Trade & Exchange) · 13 Who Gets to Be a "Real" Artist? (Amateur & Outsider Art) · 14 What's the Difference Between Art & Design? · 15 How a Banana Sold for $150,000 (Modern Art) · 16 Why Is Everyone So Mad About Public Art? · 17 Graffiti, Street Art & Murals · 18 Is AI-Generated Art Original? · 19 Why Is Art So Expensive? · 20 Does Defacing Art = Activism? · 21 Preserving, Restoring & Conserving Art · 22 What Is the Future of Art?

**Shape:** foundations (why study, how to look, museums) → value questions (greatness, the artist, good art) → functions (narrative, religion, nature, community, body) → materials and boundaries (color, outsider art, design) → contemporary controversies (market, public art, AI, activism, conservation).

## Episode anatomy
- Opens on a provocative case (the $150,000 banana) → a question → a cross-cultural sample of works as evidence → competing answers → a takeaway "way of looking."

## Takeaways for LeetLit
- **A thematic path is a strong alternative entry to the chronological survey.** LeetLit's art roadmap could offer both: a "Story of Art" timeline (Gombrich or AP) and a "Big Questions" track (this series).
- **"How to Look at Art" as episode 2** again places a looking-skills node at the root.
